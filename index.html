Question 02
Which area of life drains your tank the fastest?
Work. Parenting. Marriage. Waiting. Sickness. Loneliness. The area that drains you most — is the area where your identity is most invested. That's where the enemy goes first.
He doesn't attack where you are strong.
He attacks where you care the most.
Because that's where the wound goes deepest.

Where your identity is invested —
a failure isn't just a mistake.
It becomes a verdict on who you are.

Share with your group —
Which area drains you the most?
And in that area — what does the enemy whisper to you?
